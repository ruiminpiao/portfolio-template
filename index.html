About Me

Hi, my name is Ruimin Piao, but you can also call me Min. Currently, I'm a Multimedia and Graphic Design student at UDIT (Madrid).

I love creating visual designs using different styles and aims, and what I'm most interested in is editorial design. Besides, I'm adaptable, organized and always up for a new challenge.

My goal is to become an art director or to work in the publishing world, creating new visuals that combine innovative aesthetics and emotions.

I'm thrilled to grow as a Graphic Designer and to learn new things, so thank you for checking out my portfolio, I really hope you enjoyed it!
