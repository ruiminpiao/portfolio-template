By following the visual aesthetics of his latest released music videos, this lyrics book uses a more experimental layout mixed with the use of simple illustrations to express the storyline of the artist’s music and everything that he wants to convey through it.

INSIDE THE LYRICS PT.2

(Sep 2025, Illustration)

These is a duo of illustrated posters for two of the most popular festivals (Dragon Boat and Mid-Autumn Festival) that are celebrated in China.

Because of the importance that these festivities play in Chinese culture, the posters aim to promote part of that culture. Therefore, it uses dark but vibrant colors to create a great mix between traditional features and contemporary elements.

ANIMAYO '26

(Oct 2025, Packaging)

This is a collection of three candles which main source of inspiration is longing and pride for one’s hometown.

In order to make the design more relatable, the composition takes a much more casual style combined with little details from Chinese culture, creating like this, also a perfect image of how this Asian country looks like nowadays, as it mixes a lot of traditional and modern aesthetics all around.

BACK IN CHINA

(Oct 2025, Branding)

This is a coffee shop that also serves as a little space for readers, by not only serving different drinks, but also selling books in a space that has been created for readers.

Because of the different types of book genres and drinks that exist, the brand identity uses a straightforward design that without using many elements, conveys the combination of the personaities from coffee drinkers and bookworms.
